# When to Aerate a Lawn for the Best Recovery

**Quick answer:** Core-aerate compacted turf while its grass is actively growing and has enough moisture to recover—usually early fall for cool-season lawns and late spring into early summer for warm-season lawns.

For when to aerate a lawn for the best recovery, this guide is designed for a homeowner who wants a defensible result, not a shortcut that merely looks convincing for one afternoon. Start with what is visible, keep safety controls intact, and let the response of the turf, plant, compost, or tool determine the next move.
