### Can I use a stronger setting to compensate?

With wet packed clippings in mind, only when the manual permits that setting and the rest of the system is rated for it. Extra pressure, speed, torque or runtime can move the symptom elsewhere and accelerate wear without correcting the cause.

For how to clean under a lawn mower deck safely, repeat the original test under comparable conditions, then complete one normal work cycle. A real fix improves the result consistently without creating leakage, heat, vibration, runoff, plant stress or another new symptom.
