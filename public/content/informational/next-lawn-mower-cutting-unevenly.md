## Frequently asked questions

### Should I replace the equipment as soon as this problem appears?

For why is my lawn mower cutting unevenly, usually not. Establish a baseline and rule out cleaning, setup, compatibility and normal wear first. Replace the affected item when inspection shows damage, it cannot meet the documented workload, or the manufacturer does not support a safe repair.

### Can I use a stronger setting to compensate?

With unequal wheel height in mind, only when the manual permits that setting and the rest of the system is rated for it. Extra pressure, speed, torque or runtime can move the symptom elsewhere and accelerate wear without correcting the cause.

### How can I tell whether the fix worked?

For why is my lawn mower cutting unevenly, repeat the original test under comparable conditions, then complete one normal work cycle. A real fix improves the result consistently without creating leakage, heat, vibration, runoff, plant stress or another new symptom.
